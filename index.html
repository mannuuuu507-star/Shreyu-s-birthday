FOR MY SHREYU

Shreyu, my one and only safe place.

I can literally write a lot for you—when I say a lot, I mean a lot.

Your arrival in my life was so unexpected, almost like God himself sent me you. I never expected to find love, and little did I know that you would become so important to me from the very beginning.

I don't know how, but I just kept falling for you more and more after we got into a relationship.

I became someone I wanted to be.
A yapper who finally had someone who listened to me, and someone I wanted to talk to as much as I could.

Love, I'm so lucky to have you in my life. You are one of the most precious and prettiest parts of my life.

You met me at a point when I didn't even want to meet someone, but somehow, we still found each other.

You are that little shine without which I feel like I can't shine the same way. You are the person I look for early in the morning. I check my phone repeatedly, and somehow, you find your way into almost every thought—and obviously, into my heart.

Thank you for loving me the way you do.
And you are the best, Shreyansh. 🤍

Sometimes I wonder what my life would look like if I had never met you.

You make me happy—so happieeee 😭—like my inner child keeps showing up whenever I'm with you.

I have become a babyyy who literally wants to tell you everything. And the way you talk to me in your babyy-babyy language is honestly one of the best things ever. 😭❤️

I just love every single thing about you, every little thing, Shrey. 😭❤️

From the childish Shreyu to the mature Shreyu, every version of you is alive in my heart.

I just wannaaa live those dreams with you.
I just wanna be with you in every single life.
May we find each other in every single universe. 🌙

I hope God keeps us like this always—
a little mature and a little childish,
a little understanding and a little clumsy. ❤️😭🥺

And may He heal every little fight between us and always bring us back to understanding each other.

Thank you for being you.

For being my Shreyanshh, my Shrey, my listener, my boyyyy, my little babygirllll, and my soulmate. ❤️

Thank you for coming into my life. I love you more than you could ever imagine.

Thank you for treating me the way you do, for being the way you are— so soft, so gentle, so mineeee. 😭❤️

I love you. 😭❤️

A genuine wish—not just for this year, but for every year of your life.

May God always keep you happy, make you stronger, and let you stay a little childish too. Because I love you so, so, so much that I just want to see you as a happy child—the happiest child in the world. 😭❤️

And when it comes to maturity, you are mature enough, and I genuinely love how mature you are. Thank you for being the way you are.

I guess with age, some things change, and if there's one thing I want to remain the same in you, it's the way you are childish with me.

I want to see us after many, many years, still the same way we are now. ❤️

May success always find you. May you reach heights you never even imagined. May all your dreams come true.

And most importantly, may you always be happy, because your happiness matters to me more than you know.

Your laugh means a lot to me. So, to keep you happy, I'm always here. Always. I just want to see you as the happiest man in the world. 🫂❤️

May God always protect you, protect you from every evil eye, and always give you the strength and wisdom to handle even the hardest situations in life. 🌙

And there's one thing I want you to remember whenever you read this:

There will always be one person in your life who is with you—me, your Muskan.

There may be situations in the future where we won't be able to talk. I don't know what the future holds, but I know that you will always have a place in my heart, and I will always keep praying for good things for you.

So just keep this in mind—

Your Muskan is always with you.

Physically or from far away, I'll always be here, cheering for you, praying for you, and wishing the very best for you. ❤️🫂

HAPPIEST BIRTHDAY, MY SHREYU! ❤️🫂

To someone very special to me—always be the way you are.

May Almighty always protect you.
You deserve everything beautiful, and I would never leave a chance to remind you of that. 🌷🌙

I LOVE YOUUU, MERI JAAN. ❤️🥺

HAPPIEST BIRTHDAY, ANSHH! ❤️

~ Yours, Muku ❤️
